`za.co.proplyst.app`, which matches no Play Console app.

**Play Console app:** `za.co.genbridge.proplyst`. Checked 2026-09-15: Latest releases None, Latest
app bundles None. The Android applicationId was changed to match it that day. So versionCode 1 is
unconsumed and stays 1 for the first upload.

The final signed AAB is built in a separate step:

1. Build the bundle from a clean, pushed commit.
2. Before uploading, confirm with `aapt2 dump badging` on the matching release APK (or bundletool
   for the AAB) that the package is `za.co.genbridge.proplyst` and versionCode is 1.
3. Record its size, SHA-256, signature, applicationId, versionCode and permissions here, checked the
   same way as below.
4. Play reserves a versionCode once a bundle is uploaded. Every later upload needs a higher one.

Release builds made during audits (APK or AAB) are test artefacts. They are moved out of the build
folder into a folder named `…NOT-FOR-UPLOAD` and are never uploaded.

## How these were verified

Not assumed from a successful Gradle run:

- `aapt2 dump badging` on the release APK for applicationId, versionCode, versionName, targetSdk
  and the final merged permission list
- `apksigner verify --print-certs` for the signature scheme and certificate digest
- `jarsigner -verify` and `keytool -printcert -jarfile` for the AAB
- installed on an Android 15 emulator, signed in against production, and driven through Home,
  Properties, property detail, Activity, and More → Account & security → Delete account
