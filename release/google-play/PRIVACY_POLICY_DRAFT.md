## Proplyst Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date of publication]

Proplyst ("we", "us") provides property-management software for landlords and property owners. This
policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. It covers the Proplyst website
at proplyst.co.za and the Proplyst Android application.

Proplyst is operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], [REGISTERED ADDRESS], South Africa.

### What we collect

**Your account.** Your email address and password when you register, and your name and, optionally,
your phone number. Passwords are stored hashed by our authentication provider; we never see them.

**What you put into Proplyst.** The records you create in order to manage your portfolio:
properties and their addresses, units, tenants and their contact details, leases, invoices,
payments, expenses, budgets, utility meter readings, and maintenance items. If you attach a
document or photograph — a receipt, a municipal bill, evidence of a repair — we store that file.

**Information about tenants and other people.** Proplyst lets you record details about your
tenants. Where you do that, you are responsible for having a lawful basis to hold that information,
and we process it on your behalf and on your instructions.

**Technical information.** Ordinary server logs generated when your device contacts our servers,
including IP address and timestamps, kept for security and troubleshooting.

**What we do not collect.** Proplyst contains no advertising, analytics, attribution or
crash-reporting software. We do not track you across other apps or websites, we do not build
advertising profiles, and we do not collect your location, contacts, calendar, messages or browsing
history. The Android app requests no camera or storage permission: photographs are attached through
your device's own camera app and file picker, so Proplyst never has open access to your gallery.

### Why we use it

- To provide the service and show you your own records.
- To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
- To send you service messages you have asked for, such as rent reminders and payment
  confirmations, including by WhatsApp where you have configured it.
- To meet our legal obligations, including retention of financial records.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

### Who processes it for us

- **Supabase** — database, file storage and authentication.
- **Render** — application hosting.
- **Meta Platforms** — delivery of WhatsApp messages, where you enable them.
- **Resend** — delivery of email.
- **PayFast** — subscription payments for Proplyst itself, on the website. Card details are
  handled by PayFast and never reach us.

Each acts only on our instructions. [Confirm whether any of these process data outside South Africa
and state it here.]

### How we protect it

Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Access is enforced at the database level by row-level
security, so one organisation cannot read another's records. On Android, your session is stored in
the device's encrypted storage and is excluded from Android's automatic backups, so it cannot be
restored onto another device.

### How long we keep it

We keep your records for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account we remove
your name, email address and phone number and permanently disable sign-in.

We retain accounting records — invoices, payments, expenses and the audit trail — for five years,
because South African tax law requires financial records to be kept for that period. Those records
sit with the organisation that owns them and no longer identify you personally.

### Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time:

- In the Android app: **More → Account → Delete account**.
- On the web: **https://proplyst.co.za/delete-account**, which also explains what happens if you can
  no longer sign in.

### Your rights

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) you may ask us for a copy of the personal
information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or object to how we use it. Contact us at
[SUPPORT EMAIL].

If you believe we have not handled your information properly you may complain to the Information
Regulator of South Africa.

### Children

Proplyst is business software and is not directed at children under 18.
