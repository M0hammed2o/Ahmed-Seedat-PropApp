## Full description (4000 char max)
```
Proplyst is property management software for South African landlords and property owners. It keeps
the money side of a rental portfolio in one place, on your phone.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Portfolio at a glance
See rent collected, rent still outstanding, operating expenses and your monthly net position for
the current month, across every property you own. Occupancy and property counts sit alongside them,
so you know where you stand without opening a spreadsheet.

Needs attention
Proplyst watches your portfolio and surfaces what actually needs you: rent that has gone overdue,
budgets that are close to or over their limit, unusual water or electricity usage, leases coming up
for renewal, and maintenance that has been open too long. Alerts are grouped and ranked, never an
endless list.

Properties and units
Every property, its units, occupancy, and the money each one brings in. Open a property to see rent
planned, collected and outstanding, total expenses and its operating position.

Rent
See who has paid, who has partly paid and who is overdue, month by month. Record a payment against
an invoice, or confirm a payment a tenant has reported.

Expenses
Record an expense against a property or a specific unit, choose a category, and attach a receipt
from your camera, your gallery or your files.

Budgets
Set a monthly operating budget per property and watch actual spend against it, with the amount
remaining and a clear on-track, approaching or over-budget state.

Utilities
Capture water and electricity meter readings. Proplyst works out consumption from the previous
reading and flags unusual usage so you can investigate before it becomes a bill.

Maintenance
Track maintenance from reported through to complete, with the detail attached.

WHO IT IS FOR

Proplyst is built for owners and landlords managing their own portfolio, from a single flat to
several buildings. It uses South African conventions throughout: rands, municipal rates and taxes,
body corporate levies, and prepaid or metered utilities.

Proplyst also runs in your browser at proplyst.co.za. The app and the web application share the
same account and the same data.

A Proplyst account is required.
```
