# Risk Register

Every risk is scored **Likelihood** (Low/Medium/High) × **Impact** (Low/Medium/High/Critical) → **Severity** (the combination), with a mitigation and status. "Status" is `Open` (unmitigated), `Mitigated` (a documented mitigation exists, residual risk accepted), or `Monitoring` (no action needed now, watched for a trigger condition). This register is reviewed and updated at every major milestone (`TASKS.md`), not written once and forgotten.
